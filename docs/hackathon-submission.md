# Hackathon submission notes

This file prepares project text for the challenge form described in the supplied requirements. It does not submit, save, lock, or publish anything to the challenge website.

## Project summary draft

ASTRA is a local-first astronaut health-monitoring prototype for long-duration missions. Separate modules help crew review cardiovascular and respiratory indicators, sleep, activity, voluntary wellness, radiation records, environmental conditions, assigned equipment, personal trends and medical-review prompts. Each measurement preserves its timestamp, source, quality and data class. ASTRA SENTINEL independently monitors incoming records and source freshness; synthetic health patterns are scored only in the simulator. A source-cited assistant retrieves authorized health context and curated space-health references. The research catalog distinguishes NASA study resources from live feeds and from terrestrial clinical data. ASTRA is a research prototype: seeded values are synthetic, immune and bone-health integrations remain research scaffolds, device services are not connected, and the app is not a certified medical device or flight-qualified system.

## Project details draft

ASTRA provides a mission selection page followed by astronaut workspaces and dedicated health domains. A role-scoped mission dashboard supports assigned medical officers and administrators. Health, environmental and equipment samples are validated and saved to SQLite with provenance. ASTRA SENTINEL is an independent background service for data quality, source-local statistical change, equipment state and stale-stream review; its findings can be opened, acknowledged or resolved. The synthetic Isolation Forest is restricted to simulated health telemetry. The assistant searches local references and authorized records, cites retrieved sources and falls back to local answers when no language-model credential is configured. Simulation scenarios cover health, environmental, wearable and equipment behavior. CSV/JSON health import, custom history windows, CSV export, reports and offline caching complete the working prototype.

ASTRA was built with Node.js 24, built-in SQLite, browser JavaScript/CSS, a custom Isolation Forest implementation and local lexical retrieval. The design responds to isolation, communication delay, limited onboard resources and data provenance constraints by keeping core monitoring local and making synthetic, imported and unavailable values visually distinct. NASA HRP risk topics inform the module structure; the local summary is a reference, not an official NASA dataset or medical rule. Bone and immune modules are deliberately shown as unconnected research scaffolds. The project is not validated for operational medical use.

## Super Intelligence disclosure draft

AI tools assisted with ASTRA's source code, interface text, technical documentation and local reference summaries. Demonstration astronaut profiles, health readings, radiation values, and ML training/evaluation examples are synthetic and generated by code. No AI-generated images, video or audio are included. No NASA logos, flags, mission insignia or other NASA visual branding are used.
